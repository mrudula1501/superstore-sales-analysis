# Superstore Sales Analysis Report

## Executive Summary
Analyzed 9,800 orders from a US retail superstore (2015–2018) to identify sales trends, regional performance, and top customers.

## Key Findings
1. **West region** leads in total sales (~$725K), followed by East (~$678K)
2. **Technology** is the top-selling category ($827K), followed by Furniture ($728K)
3. Sales show consistent **year-over-year growth** from $479K (2015) to $722K (2018)
4. **Sean Miller** is the top customer with ~$25K in purchases

## Recommendations
1. Invest more in West and East regions for maximum ROI
2. Expand Technology product lines — highest revenue category
3. Focus retention efforts on top 10 customers who drive significant revenue

## Limitations
- Dataset covers only 4 years; longer trends would improve forecasting
- No profit margin data available — high sales may not mean high profit
- Geographic analysis limited to region level, not city/state

## Conclusion
The Superstore shows strong growth momentum. Strategic focus on the West region and Technology category, combined with top-customer retention, can further accelerate revenue.
